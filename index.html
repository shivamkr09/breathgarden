Breathgarden is a night garden that moves with your breathing. Breathe slowly with the guide ring, and flowers grow.

What it uses

  • Your camera, only while a session is running. You’ll see a Camera on badge and your preview the whole time.
  • Breathing movement and rate, plus pulse rate and HRV, estimated from the video by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK.

Where it goes

  • Video frames are processed on this computer. No video is uploaded or recorded.
  • Breathgarden saves nothing. Signals stay in memory and are erased when you tap Forget or close the app.
  • The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key. Optional diagnostics are below, off by default.

Breathgarden is for relaxation only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Please use it only on yourself.

Privacy & data handling

  • Camera: requested only after you accept the consent screen and press Begin. The app grants itself video only (never the microphone), and only to its own bundled page.
  • Processing: frames go from this window to the SmartSpectra engine in the app’s own process, on this computer. Breathgarden never uploads, records or screenshots video.
  • Storage: none. Breathing trace, rates, pulse and HRV live in memory for the current session. Forget clears them, and closing the app clears everything.
  • Network: the SDK authenticates your API key with Presage over TLS. If you turn on diagnostics, it also sends session summaries and device readiness reports. These contain SDK and device info, frame rates and error codes, and may contain technical free text. They never contain frames or measured vitals, and authenticated requests can be linked to the API key’s account. They are kept under Presage’s policies.
  • AI services: none. Breathgarden does not use LLM Insights or any other AI service.
  • Not medical: relaxation only. No diagnosis, treatment or monitoring.
