Privacy & data handling

  • Camera: requested only after you accept the consent step and press Continue. The app grants itself video only (never the microphone), and only to its own bundled page. A “Camera on” badge and a preview are shown whenever it is on.
  • Processing: frames go from this window to the SmartSpectra engine in the app’s own process, on this computer. Breathgarden never uploads or records video.
  • Storage: none by default. Breathing trace, rates, pulse and HRV live in memory for the current session. Forget clears them, and closing the app clears everything. Save postcard writes only the garden picture to a file you choose: hills shaped by your breathing pattern, the date, minutes and flower count. It contains no numbers or readings.
  • Network: the SDK checks your API key with Presage over TLS. If you turn on diagnostics, it also sends session summaries and readiness reports (SDK and device info, frame rates, error codes, possibly technical free text). They never contain frames or measured vitals, but they can be linked to the API key’s account. Retention follows Presage’s policies.
  • Talking: the SDK’s talking signal only pauses the garden while you speak. No audio is captured; the microphone is never used.
  • Voice guide: optional, off by default. It plays short clips that were generated once with ElevenLabs text-to-speech from fixed phrases (no personal data), or your computer’s built-in voice. Nothing is sent anywhere during a session, and it never speaks your readings.
  • Sound garden: optional, off by default. The music is synthesized on this computer from your signals; nothing is recorded or sent.
  • AI services: none.
  • Not medical: relaxation only. No diagnosis, treatment or monitoring.
